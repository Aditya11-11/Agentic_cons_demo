SECTION 1: ACCOUNT & LOGIN ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Forgotten Password
If you cannot log in because you forgot your password, go to the login page and
click "Forgot Password". Enter the email address associated with your account.
You will receive a password reset email within 2–5 minutes. Check your spam or
junk folder if it does not arrive. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If
it has expired, simply request a new one from the login page.

## Account Locked After Failed Attempts
Your account is temporarily locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
The lock lasts 15 minutes automatically. After 15 minutes, you may try again.
If you believe your account has been compromised, contact support immediately to
initiate a security review. Do not share your password with anyone.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Not Working
If your 2FA code is rejected, first ensure your device's time is synchronized
correctly — authenticator apps are time-sensitive. Go to your device settings,
find Date & Time, and enable automatic time synchronization. If the issue
persists, you can use one of your backup codes provided when you set up 2FA. To
find backup codes, log in via a trusted device, go to Settings > Security >
Two-Factor Authentication > View Backup Codes. If you have lost all backup
codes, contact support with your account email and photo ID for identity
verification.

## Email Not Recognized at Login
If the system does not recognize your email, you may have signed up with a
different email address, or your account may have been deleted. Try any
alternate email addresses you use. If you signed up through Google or GitHub
SSO (Single Sign-On), use the "Continue with Google" or "Continue with GitHub"
button instead of email/password. To check all emails associated with your
account, email verify@techflow.io from any email you think you used.

## Changing Your Email Address
To change the email on your account, go to Settings > Profile > Email. You will
be sent a confirmation link to your new email address. The change takes effect
only after you click the confirmation link. Your old email address will receive
a security notification. If you did not initiate this change, contact support
immediately.

SECTION 2: BILLING & SUBSCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## How Billing Works
TechFlow bills monthly or annually depending on your plan. Monthly plans are
billed on the same date each month (your billing date). Annual plans are billed
once per year upfront and receive a 20% discount compared to monthly pricing.
All prices are in USD. Taxes may apply depending on your country of residence.

## Upgrading Your Plan
To upgrade, go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Select your new plan and
confirm. Upgrades take effect immediately. You will be charged a prorated amount
for the remainder of your current billing period. For example, if you upgrade
halfway through a monthly billing cycle, you will only pay for the remaining
days at the new plan rate.

## Downgrading Your Plan
Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period — you will
retain full access to your current plan until then. Go to Settings > Billing >
Change Plan and select the lower tier. You will see the date the downgrade
takes effect. No refunds are issued for unused time on a higher plan.

## Cancelling a Subscription
To cancel, go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Follow the prompts.
Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. After
cancellation, your data is retained for 90 days in case you wish to reactivate.
After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted. To reactivate within 90 days,
go to the login page and select "Reactivate Account".

## Refund Policy
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions. If you are not
satisfied within the first 14 days of a new subscription, contact support at
billing@techflow.io with your account email and reason. Refunds are processed
within 5–7 business days back to your original payment method. Refunds are not
available after the 14-day window, for plan renewals, or for add-ons.

## Payment Failed / Card Declined
If your payment fails, you will receive an email notification. You have a 7-day
grace period to update your payment method before your account is suspended. To
update your card, go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods > Add New Card.
Common reasons for payment failure include: expired card, insufficient funds,
bank fraud prevention block, or incorrect billing address. If you updated your
card and the charge still fails, contact your bank to confirm there is no block
on international or subscription transactions.

## Invoice and Receipt Download
All invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoice History. Click
any invoice to download a PDF receipt. If you need an invoice reissued with
specific company details (for expense reporting), email billing@techflow.io
with your account email and the required details.

SECTION 3: TECHNICAL ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## App Not Loading / Blank Screen
If the app shows a blank screen or fails to load:
1. Hard-refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
3. Try an incognito/private window.
4. Disable browser extensions one at a time to identify any conflict.
5. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
If the issue persists across browsers, check our status page at
status.techflow.io to see if there is an ongoing incident.

## Slow Performance / High Load Time
Slow performance can be caused by: a large number of records in your workspace,
browser memory overload, or a network issue. Try closing other browser tabs,
restarting your browser, or checking your internet speed at fast.com. If the
platform is consistently slow only for your account, contact support — it may
indicate a data indexing issue specific to your workspace.

## File Upload Failing
Supported file formats: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, TXT, PNG, JPG, MP4 (for media).
Maximum file size: 50 MB per file, 500 MB per workspace per month on the
Starter plan, unlimited on Pro and Enterprise. If your upload is failing:
1. Check the file format and size.
2. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
3. Try a different browser.
4. If the file is exactly the right format but still fails, it may be corrupted.
   Try re-exporting or re-saving it.

## Integration Not Syncing (Slack, Jira, GitHub)
If a third-party integration stops syncing:
1. Go to Settings > Integrations and click the affected integration.
2. Click "Re-authorize" to refresh the connection token.
3. Check that the connected account still has the correct permissions in the
   third-party app (e.g., the connected Slack workspace admin may have revoked
   bot permissions).
4. Disconnect and reconnect the integration.
If syncing still fails after reconnecting, check the integration logs under
Settings > Integrations > [Name] > Activity Log for error codes and share them
with support.

## API Errors (for Developers)
Common API error codes:
- 401 Unauthorized: Your API key is missing, invalid, or expired. Regenerate
  your key at Settings > Developer > API Keys.
- 403 Forbidden: Your API key does not have permission for the resource. Check
  the scope assigned to your key.
- 429 Too Many Requests: You have hit the rate limit. Starter: 100 req/min,
  Pro: 1000 req/min, Enterprise: unlimited. Implement exponential backoff.
- 500 Internal Server Error: A server-side issue. Check status.techflow.io and
  retry after a few minutes.

## Data Not Saving / Changes Lost
If changes are not saving, check:
1. Your network connection — offline mode is not supported.
2. Whether you see an error toast notification (yellow/red banner) — this
   usually contains the reason.
3. Whether your session has expired — the app logs you out after 8 hours of
   inactivity. Log back in and retry.
4. If saving consistently fails on a specific record, that record may be
   corrupted. Export it, delete it, and reimport.

SECTION 4: FEATURES & HOW-TO GUIDES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## How to Create a Project
1. From the Dashboard, click "+ New Project" in the top right.
2. Enter a project name (required) and description (optional).
3. Choose a template (Blank, Agile Board, CRM, or Support Desk) or start blank.
4. Set visibility: Private (only invited members) or Team (all workspace members).
5. Click "Create Project". You will be taken to the project board automatically.

## How to Invite Team Members
Go to Settings > Workspace > Members > Invite Members. Enter their email
address and select a role:
- Viewer: Read-only access to all projects.
- Editor: Can create and edit items, cannot delete projects.
- Admin: Full access including billing and member management.
Invitees receive an email invitation valid for 48 hours. Uninvited members can
be re-invited from the same screen. You can also share a workspace invite link
from Settings > Workspace > Members > Copy Invite Link.

## How to Export Data
Go to Settings > Data > Export. Choose the format (CSV or JSON) and the
scope (entire workspace, a specific project, or a date range). Large exports are
processed in the background — you will receive an email with a download link
when ready. Export links expire after 7 days.

## How to Set Up Automations
Automations trigger actions when conditions are met (e.g., "When status changes
to Done, notify Slack"). To create one:
1. Open a Project > click "Automations" in the sidebar.
2. Click "+ New Automation".
3. Set the Trigger (e.g., Status Changed, Item Created, Due Date Reached).
4. Add one or more Actions (Send Notification, Assign Member, Move Item, etc.).
5. Toggle the automation on and click Save.
Automations run within ~30 seconds of the trigger event. The Starter plan
allows up to 10 active automations per workspace. Pro and Enterprise are
unlimited.

## How to Use the Search Function
The global search bar (top of every page, or press Ctrl+K / Cmd+K) searches
across all projects, items, comments, and attachments in your workspace. Use
quotation marks for exact phrase search: "Q3 report". Use the filter icon next
to the search bar to limit results by project, type, date, or assignee.

## Setting Up Notifications
Go to Settings > Notifications. You can configure:
- Email notifications: Daily digest, instant, or off.
- In-app notifications: What events trigger a bell notification.
- Slack/Teams: Get notified in a channel (requires integration).
You can also set per-project notification preferences from inside each project
under Project Settings > Notifications.

SECTION 5: ENTERPRISE & SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Single Sign-On (SSO) Setup
SSO is available on the Enterprise plan. Supported providers: Okta, Azure AD,
Google Workspace, and custom SAML 2.0 providers. To configure:
1. Go to Settings > Security > Single Sign-On.
2. Download the Service Provider metadata XML.
3. Upload it to your Identity Provider (IdP) to create the SAML app.
4. Copy the IdP SSO URL and certificate back into TechFlow's SSO settings.
5. Test with a pilot user before enforcing SSO for all members.
Contact enterprise@techflow.io if you need assistance with setup.

## Data Residency
Enterprise customers can choose their data residency region:
- US (default): US-East (Virginia)
- EU: eu-west-1 (Ireland)
- APAC: ap-southeast-1 (Singapore)
Data residency must be configured before workspace creation — it cannot be
changed after the fact. Contact enterprise@techflow.io to request a region-
specific workspace.

## GDPR and Data Deletion Requests
For GDPR data subject access requests (DSAR) or deletion requests, email
privacy@techflow.io with the subject line "DSAR Request" or "Data Deletion
Request". Include the user's email address and workspace name. We respond
within 30 days in compliance with GDPR Article 15/17. For bulk deletion (e.g.,
removing a former employee's data), Workspace Admins can delete individual user
data from Settings > Members > [Member] > Delete Data.

## Audit Logs
Audit logs are available on the Pro and Enterprise plans. Go to Settings >
Security > Audit Logs. Logs track: logins, permission changes, data exports,
API key creation, and member invitations. Logs are retained for 90 days on Pro
and 365 days on Enterprise. Logs can be exported as CSV or streamed to a SIEM
via webhook (Enterprise only).

## Example Ticket 1
Issue: Cannot log in, forgot password, reset email not received.
Resolution: Advised user to check spam folder. Reset email was in spam. User
accessed account successfully. Root cause: Gmail spam filter flagged the
no-reply@techflow.io domain. Advised user to whitelist the domain.

## Example Ticket 2
Issue: Payment failed, card declined.
Resolution: User's bank had flagged the recurring subscription as suspicious.
Advised user to call their bank and authorize the transaction. User confirmed
with bank, payment processed on second attempt. Account restored.

## Example Ticket 3
Issue: File upload failing for large PDF (45 MB).
Resolution: User was on Starter plan with 50 MB file limit. File was 45 MB
but also had embedded video content that was not supported. Advised user to
remove embedded video and re-export. Upload succeeded at 12 MB.

## Example Ticket 4
Issue: 2FA codes not working.
Resolution: User's device clock was 4 minutes out of sync. Advised enabling
automatic time sync. After sync, 2FA codes accepted immediately.

## Example Ticket 5
Issue: Slack integration stopped sending notifications.
Resolution: The Slack bot token had expired after a Slack workspace admin
rotation. User re-authorized the integration under Settings > Integrations >
Slack. Notifications resumed within 2 minutes.

## Example Ticket 6
Issue: API returning 429 errors despite low usage.
Resolution: User was on Starter plan (100 req/min) but had implemented a
polling loop calling the API every 500ms, exceeding the rate limit. Advised
implementing webhook-based updates instead of polling and adding exponential
backoff. User updated implementation and errors resolved.

## Example Ticket 7
Issue: Team member invitation not received.
Resolution: Invite email was blocked by the recipient's corporate email filter.
Advised sharing the workspace invite link directly (Settings > Workspace >
Members > Copy Invite Link) as an alternative. Member joined successfully.

## Example Ticket 8
Issue: User wants to cancel but retain data.
Resolution: Explained that data is retained for 90 days after cancellation.
User confirmed cancellation. Sent a data export link before cancellation to
ensure user had a copy of all project data.

## Example Ticket 9
Issue: User upgraded from Starter to Pro but workspace still shows Starter limits.
Resolution: Plan change was confirmed in billing but a cache issue prevented the
UI from refreshing. Advised user to log out and log back in. After re-login, the
Pro plan features and limits appeared correctly. No billing discrepancy found.

## Example Ticket 10
Issue: Data export requested but download link expired before user could access it.
Resolution: The export link was valid for 7 days but the user did not check email
in time. Re-triggered the export from Settings > Data > Export. New link sent to
user's verified email. Advised user to download within 7 days. Export completed.

## Example Ticket 11
Issue: User cannot find audit logs, receives "Feature not available" message.
Resolution: User was on the Starter plan which does not include audit logs.
Explained that audit logs are available on Pro and Enterprise plans only.
Provided upgrade instructions via Settings > Billing > Change Plan. User chose
to upgrade to Pro. Audit logs became accessible immediately after upgrade.

## Example Ticket 12
Issue: Automation rule not firing — "When status changes to Done, notify Slack".
Resolution: The user had reached the 10-automation limit on the Starter plan.
The automation was saved but remained in a disabled state. Advised the user to
either delete an unused automation or upgrade to Pro for unlimited automations.
User deleted two old automations and re-enabled the Slack notification rule.
Automation fired correctly on next status change.

## Example Ticket 13
Issue: GitHub integration showing "Permission Denied" after organization transfer.
Resolution: The GitHub repository was transferred to a new organization, which
invalidated the existing integration token. Advised user to disconnect the GitHub
integration under Settings > Integrations > GitHub > Disconnect, then reconnect
with the new organization's permissions. User completed the steps and syncing
resumed within 5 minutes.

## Example Ticket 14
Issue: User's workspace data appears to be missing after browser update.
Resolution: The browser update cleared all cached data including local session
tokens. User was not actually logged in — the dashboard was loading a cached
shell. Advised user to log back in with credentials. All workspace data was
intact on the server. Confirmed no data loss occurred.

## Example Ticket 15
Issue: SSO login failing with "SAML Response Invalid" error.
Resolution: The customer's Azure AD certificate had expired during their annual
rotation. Advised the IT admin to re-export the certificate from Azure AD and
re-upload it under Settings > Security > Single Sign-On. After certificate update,
SSO login resumed for all workspace members within 10 minutes.

## Example Ticket 16
Issue: User receiving duplicate email notifications for every project update.
Resolution: User had both "Instant" email notifications and "Daily Digest"
enabled simultaneously, plus per-project overrides that also sent instant emails.
Advised disabling the global instant notifications at Settings > Notifications
and keeping only the daily digest. Duplicates stopped immediately.

## Example Ticket 17
Issue: CSV export contains garbled characters for non-English project names.
Resolution: The user was opening the CSV file in Excel which defaults to ANSI
encoding. TechFlow exports in UTF-8. Advised user to open Excel, use Data >
From Text/CSV, and select UTF-8 encoding during import. Characters displayed
correctly after re-import.

## Example Ticket 18
Issue: User accidentally deleted a project and wants to recover it.
Resolution: Deleted projects are moved to Trash and retained for 30 days before
permanent deletion. Guided user to Settings > Workspace > Trash. User found the
project and clicked "Restore". Project and all associated data were restored to
the workspace within seconds.
